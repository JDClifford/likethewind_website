Selected work

Sculpture & form

Ceramic sculpture

Unicorn out of Captivity

A reclining unicorn rising from a bed of fine detail, where myth and the natural world meet in one form. Left unglazed, so the modeling carries the piece.

Coil-built · Anagama-fired

Pomegranate

A round, seed-skinned vessel built by hand in coils and fired in a wood-burning anagama kiln, where the flame and ash settle into the surface.

Slab-built lamp

Honey Blocks

A faceted lamp assembled from slabs and pierced with a honeycomb lattice that scatters the light it holds.

Inspired by the natural world, baroque art, and mythical creatures. Forms made with simple palettes, to emphasize the textures and details in the work.

The maker

Breez Clifford

I was born and raised in Florida and took my first ceramics class in high school. I fell in love with hand-building on my very first project.

I now live in Marshall, North Carolina, and attend UNC Asheville, where I'm studying to become an elementary school art teacher.
